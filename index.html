STRESS

A pressure tactics game. Surround enemies to stress them out. Cracked pieces shatter. Capture the Crown to win.

HOW TO PLAY

STRESS is a tactical game on a 7×7 board. Capture your opponent's Crown to win, or crush their army with stress.

STRESS — THE CORE MECHANIC

At the end of each turn, every piece adjacent to one or more enemy pieces gains stress:

• Adjacent to 1 enemy → +1 stress
• Adjacent to 2+ enemies → +2 stress

Stress levels: 1 2 3 4

At stress 5, the piece shatters and is removed!

Moving a piece reduces its stress by 2 (minimum 0).

The Crown is immune to stress — it can only be captured.

PRESSURE ZONE

The center 3×3 area is the pressure zone. Pieces here gain +1 extra stress per turn. Control it at your own risk.

PIECES

★ Crown — Moves 1 square any direction. Lose it, lose the game. Immune to stress.
♦ Blade — Moves 1–2 squares in any direction (straight line, cannot jump).
▬ Shield — Moves 1 square orthogonally. Friendly pieces adjacent to a Shield get −1 stress at end of turn.
▲ Agent — Moves 1 square forward, sideways, or diag-forward (no retreat). Captures same way. Promotes to Blade or Shield at last rank.

SETUP (each player)

Back row: Blade, Shield, Blade, Crown, Blade, Shield, Blade
Front row:     Agent, Agent, Agent, Agent, Agent

CAPTURING

Move your piece onto an enemy piece to capture it.

TIMER

30 seconds per turn. If time runs out, a random piece makes a panic move!

STRATEGY TIPS

• Surround enemies with multiple pieces to stress them fast.
• Rotate your front line — move stressed pieces back, bring fresh ones forward.
• Shields are key defenders — protect them!
• The pressure zone is powerful but dangerous.
