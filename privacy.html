Privacy

The short version

Stress collects no data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the game never sends anything over the network.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, puzzles solved, your daily streak, achievements), your settings and any game in progress are saved in the app's local storage on your device, so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app, or using Settings › Reset progress, erases it.

Sharing

If you tap Share after a daily puzzle, the app hands a short text (for example "Stress daily #12 ✅ solved in 2 🔥 5-day streak", plus a link to the game) to your device's share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you.

Children

Stress is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever collects any data, this page and the App Store privacy label will say so before that version is released.
