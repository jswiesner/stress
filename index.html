STRESS

A chess-inspired board game where idle pieces crack under pressure. Move or lose them.

HOW TO PLAY

Stress is a 7×7 board game where pieces accumulate stress when they sit idle. Capture the enemy King to win!

THE STRESS MECHANIC

After each of your turns, every one of your pieces that did NOT move gains +1 stress (shown as dots beneath the piece).

● Mild   ● High   ● Panic!

At stress 3, a piece is panicking — if you don't move it on your next turn, it is removed from the board!

Moving a piece resets its stress to 0.

TIMER

You have 30 seconds per turn. If time runs out, a random piece will panic-move on its own!

STRESS ZONES

The center 3×3 area is a stress zone. Pieces in this zone gain +1 extra stress per turn — risk vs reward for controlling the center.

PIECES

♚ King — moves 1 square any direction. Lose him, lose the game.
♜ Tower — moves up to 3 squares orthogonally (straight lines).
♝ Shadow — moves up to 3 squares diagonally.
♞ Jumper — leaps in an L-shape (2+1), can jump over pieces.
♟ Soldier — moves 1 forward, captures 1 diagonally forward. Promotes to any piece (except King) upon reaching the last rank.

SETUP

Each player starts with: 1 King, 2 Towers, 2 Shadows, 2 Jumpers, 7 Soldiers.
