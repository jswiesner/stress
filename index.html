STRESS

A simple game of pressure. Push forward. Rotate before you crack. Capture the Crown.

1. Move a piece 1–2 squares in any direction. The ★ Crown moves only 1.
2. Land on an enemy to capture it.
3. After each turn, unmoved pieces past the center line gain +1 stress.
4. Moving resets stress. At stress 4, pieces shatter. ★ can't shatter.
5. Capture the ★ to win. 30s per turn.
