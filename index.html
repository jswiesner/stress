How to play

Raid your opponent's half to pile on Stress. First to 10 cracks. Or capture their Crown.

  1. Board. 7×7. The three rows nearest you are your half. The middle row is neutral.
  2. Pieces. Each side has a Crown, 2 Guards, 2 Runners and 2 Jumpers.
  3. Your turn. Move one piece. Land on an enemy piece to capture it. The first move alternates from game to game.
  4. Raid. At the start of your turn, each of your pieces in the enemy half adds 1 to your opponent's Stress.
  5. Nerve. A raider can do that 3 times. After its third hit it cracks and leaves the board. Move it back to the middle row or your half and its nerve refills. Your Crown never cracks.
  6. Win. Your opponent reaches 10 Stress, or you capture their Crown.
  7. Time limit. If nobody has won after 40 rounds, the player with less Stress wins. If it's tied, play on until someone takes another point. (If 10 more rounds pass without a hit, the side with more pieces wins.)

The pieces

Crown

One square in any direction. Lose it and you lose the game. It never cracks.

Guard

One or two squares straight. Can't jump. Captures by landing on an enemy (ring).

Runner

One or two squares diagonally. Can't jump, so the Guard here blocks one way.

Jumper

An L: two squares one way, one to the side. Hops over anything.

Raids and nerve

Blue's Jumper on e5 is raiding Red's half. If it is still there when Blue's turn starts, Red takes 1 Stress and the Jumper uses up one gold dot of nerve. With one dot left (red), its next hit is its last: pull it back to refill, or let it crack.

Hints

When you pick up a piece, its moves are marked. Amber: your piece could be captured there. Red: the move loses the game or exposes your Crown. Tap your opponent's pieces to see where they can go. You can turn hints off in the game.
