## Description (4000)

```text
Stress is a three-minute strategy duel with a lot of personality.

Raid your rival's half of the board to pile on Stress. Every raider rattles them once a turn, but raiders lose their nerve, so pull them back before they crack. First to 10 Stress loses. Or snatch their Crown and win on the spot.

Easy to learn, quick to play, surprisingly deep: think chess, cut down to a coffee break.

TEN RIVALS
Climb a ladder of ten opponents, from Pip (just happy to be here) to The Monk (the calmest player alive). Each one plays their own way, from reckless raiders to patient turtles to relentless Crown hunters, and each has three stars to win.

A FRESH PUZZLE EVERY DAY
Solve the daily puzzle, keep your streak going and share your result without spoilers. 150 more puzzles wait in three packs, from win-in-one warm-ups to win-in-three mastery. Every puzzle is proven by computer to have a forced win.

LEARN IN A MINUTE
A one-minute interactive tutorial teaches you everything by playing. No rulebook required.

CALM BY DESIGN
• No ads and no energy bars
• No move timer (unless you want a chess clock for two-player games)
• Undo any move
• Plays offline, saves automatically
• Light and dark mode
• Gentle sound, ambient music and haptics

LEVEL UP AND COLLECT
Earn XP and climb from Rattled to Zen Master. Unlock five more boards (Sakura, Matcha, Midnight, Sunset and Gold Leaf) and 16 achievements.

PLAY A FRIEND
Pass and play on one device.

Keep calm. Stress them out.
```

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Choose **Data Not Collected**. Stress has no accounts, analytics, ads or network requests. Progress lives on the device (WebView local storage) and never leaves it.

If an analytics or purchase SDK is added later, revisit this section before that build ships.
